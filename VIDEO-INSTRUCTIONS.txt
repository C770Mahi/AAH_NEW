CUSTOMIZATION:
--------------
Want to change how long each video plays?

1. Open main.html in a text editor
2. Find this line (around line 2656):
   let videoDuration = 15000;
   
3. Change 15000 to another number:
   • 10000 = 10 seconds per video
   • 20000 = 20 seconds per video
   • 30000 = 30 seconds per video

4. Save and refresh!
